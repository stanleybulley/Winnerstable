Meet Our Team

Anna

CEO & Co-Founder

Anna’s connection to music started in childhood, first with piano lessons and later developing a love for drums around age 11–12. Early exposure sparked her curiosity about how music is made, leading her to research, collaborate with friends, and learn through trial and error. She released her first song, “Who Are You”, in 2018, which solidified her passion. Key lessons include the importance of finding a supportive team, building personal relationships with producers, and staying curious and willing to experiment. Her biggest challenges were time management, transitioning from college to adult life, and balancing music with work and responsibilities.
